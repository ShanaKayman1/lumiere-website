The Science Behind Lab-Grown Diamonds

A common misconception is that lab-grown diamonds are look-alikes such as cubic zirconia or moissanite. In reality, lab-grown diamonds are 100% real diamonds—sharing the exact same crystal lattice, chemical composition, and optical fire as mined stones.

Using advanced HPHT (High Pressure, High Temperature) and CVD (Chemical Vapor Deposition) chambers, pure carbon is exposed to temperatures over 1,500°C and pressures exceeding 870,000 PSI—replicating the exact forces deep inside the Earth without mining.
