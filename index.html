The Science Behind Lab-Grown Diamonds

A common misconception is that lab-grown diamonds are "simulants" like cubic zirconia or moissanite. In reality, lab-grown diamonds are **100% real diamonds**.

Grown using advanced technological methods (such as HPHT and CVD) that replicate the extreme heat and pressure of the Earth's mantle, lab-grown diamonds possess the exact same crystal lattice structure, chemical composition, refractive index, and thermal conductivity as mined diamonds. Even professional gemological equipment cannot tell them apart because physically, optically, and chemically, they are identical.
