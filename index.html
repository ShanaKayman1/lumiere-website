100% Real Diamonds • Atom for Atom

What Makes a Lab-Grown Diamond Real?

Unlike diamond look-alikes such as cubic zirconia or moissanite, lab-grown diamonds are 100% real diamonds—sharing the exact same chemical, physical, and optical structure as mined diamonds.

The only difference is where they begin. Inside advanced growth chambers, pure carbon is exposed to temperatures above 1,500°C and pressures over 870,000 PSI—mirroring the natural conditions deep beneath the Earth’s crust without mining.

HPHT (High Pressure, High Temperature): Recreates natural geological heat and pressure around a pure diamond seed.

CVD (Chemical Vapor Deposition): Builds ultra-pure diamond crystals atom by atom from carbon-rich gas.
