The Science Behind Lab-Grown Diamonds

A common myth is that lab-grown diamonds are "simulants" like cubic zirconia or moissanite. In reality, lab-grown diamonds are 100% real diamonds—chemically, physically, and optically identical to stones mined from the Earth.

The only difference is origin. Instead of forming over billions of years underground, our diamonds are grown in controlled environments that replicate the extreme heat (over 1,500°C) and crushing pressure (870,000+ PSI) of the Earth’s mantle—producing pure crystallized carbon with zero mining disruption.

HPHT (High Pressure, High Temperature): Mimics natural geological diamond growth using intense heat and pressure on a pure diamond seed.

CVD (Chemical Vapor Deposition): Grows ultra-pure Type IIa diamond crystals layer by layer from carbon-rich plasma.
